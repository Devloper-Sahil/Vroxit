🔐 Privacy Policy

1. What Data We Collect

SubTrack AI Pro collects only the information you enter directly into the app — your subscription names, prices, renewal dates, and notes. We do not collect personally identifiable information beyond what you voluntarily provide.

If you sign in via Firebase Authentication (future feature), we store only your email address and display name as provided by Firebase Auth.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All subscription data you enter is stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it. We do not upload your financial details to any server.

3. No Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal financial data with any third party. Your subscription information is strictly private to your device and session.

4. Cookies & Session Data

SubTrack AI Pro uses browser LocalStorage (not traditional cookies) to save your theme preference, subscriptions, and budget settings between sessions. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

5. AI Features

The AI Receipt Scanner and AI Alternative Suggestions run entirely client-side with pattern matching — no receipt images or subscription details are sent to any external AI server in the current version.

6. Currency Conversion

Exchange rates are hardcoded approximations for display purposes. No external currency API requests are made that could expose your data.

7. Contact for Privacy Concerns

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: vroxit-io-devloper@outlook.com
